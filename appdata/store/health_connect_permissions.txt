Was die App anfordert (src-tauri/plugins/health/android/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml):
READ_EXERCISE, READ_EXERCISE_ROUTES, READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY, READ_HEART_RATE, READ_STEPS, READ_POWER


== Allgemein (App-Funktion / core feature) ==

WMap is a map and navigation app that also keeps the user's own tours. With Health Connect switched on (optional, off by default, under "Übersicht" > "Sicherung & Synchronisation" > "Health Connect"), WMap imports workouts that were recorded with a watch or another app and shows them under "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet": the route on the map, and charts along the route. WMap only reads. Nothing is written to Health Connect, nothing is uploaded; the data stays on the device (or in a folder the user chose). No account, no ads, no analytics.


== Exercise (READ_EXERCISE) ==

Purpose: find the user's recorded workouts (walk, run, hike, bike ride, rowing ...) so they can be imported as recorded tours. WMap reads start, end, type and title of each exercise session, and the pedalling cadence of bike rides.
Where in the app: "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet" – each imported workout is one entry with date, activity type, distance and duration.
Benefit: workouts recorded with a watch appear on the map next to the tours recorded with WMap itself, without exporting files by hand.


== Exercise routes (READ_EXERCISE_ROUTES) ==

Purpose: read the GPS route of an imported workout.
Where in the app: the route is drawn on the map under "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet", with distance, speed along the route, laps and elevation profile. A recorded tour can be navigated again ("Erneut navigieren").
Benefit: without the route a workout cannot be shown on a map at all – the route is the core of the feature.


== Health data history (READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY) ==

Purpose: import workouts that were recorded more than 30 days before the user granted access.
Where in the app: "Sicherung & Synchronisation" > "Health Connect" > import; the older workouts then appear under "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet", grouped by year.
Benefit: users get their complete tour history from past years, not only the last 30 days.


== Heart rate (READ_HEART_RATE) ==

Purpose: show the heart rate during an imported workout.
Where in the app: "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet" > open a workout: chart "Puls" along the route (average and maximum) and the average heart rate per lap.
Benefit: the user sees where on the route the effort was high or low.
Read only for the time span of an imported workout.


== Steps cadence (READ_STEPS – data type StepsCadence) ==

Purpose: show the step cadence (steps per minute) during an imported walk, run or hike.
Where in the app: "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet" > open a workout: chart "Schrittfrequenz" along the route (for rowing workouts the same chart is labelled "Schlagfrequenz").
Benefit: the user sees how the cadence changed over the route, e.g. uphill or towards the end of a run.
WMap reads the cadence samples only for the time span of an imported workout. It does not read or show daily step counts.


== Power (READ_POWER) ==

Purpose: show the power output in watts during an imported workout that was recorded with a power meter (bike with power meter, rowing machine, running power).
Where in the app: "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet" > open such a workout: the figure "Ø Leistung" (average watts) and the chart "Leistung" along the route, with average and maximum.
Benefit: cyclists and rowers see where on the route they produced how much power.
Note for review: figure and chart only appear if the workout in Health Connect contains power samples. A workout without power data shows neither – the test device needs a workout with route and power data (e.g. a bike ride recorded with a power meter).
Read only for the time span of an imported workout.


== Hinweis an die Prüfung (falls ein Freitextfeld da ist) ==

All charts are under "Meine Touren" > "Aufgezeichnet" > open an imported workout. A chart is only offered when the workout contains that kind of data, so the test device needs a workout with a GPS route plus heart rate, cadence and power samples in Health Connect. Speed is no longer requested as of version 2.3.0.
